# Share Test

A quiet, flat look: dark ink on warm paper, one red mark for the point that matters, and shapes that slide in and settle.

## Essence

Large margins, flat fills, no shadows and no gradients. A frame holds one idea: a title, two or three labelled shapes, and at most one thing in red. It is easily confused with a slide deck; what sets it apart is that every element arrives by motion, never by a cut.

## Colour

`ground` is the whole frame. `ink` draws every line, shape outline and word. `accent` marks the single point the narration is making at that moment, and nothing else. Nothing is filled with `ink`; shapes are outlined, or filled with a light tint of `ground`.

## Type

Inter throughout. Titles in semibold at about a tenth of the frame's height, labels in regular at about a quarter of that. Text is left aligned to a margin of one tenth of the frame width and never set in all capitals.

## Motion

Elements enter by sliding up a short distance while fading in, over `enter` (0.6 s) on the `arrive` curve, one after another about 0.15 s apart, in reading order. The red mark draws on last, when the narration names its point. Nothing moves once it has settled; an element leaves by fading out over the same duration.

## Avoid

More than one red element on screen; it stops meaning anything. Bouncing or overshooting easing. Filling the frame edge to edge; keep the margins empty.
