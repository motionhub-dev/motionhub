## Inspiration

Style only. No asset, frame, logo, brand, code or composition was copied.

- https://x.com/rackfocusfx/status/2119783851211788513 by @rackfocusfx. A blue-and-green promotional register — infrastructure diagrams, interface tables and kinetic type in one palette — read as a direction for the palette and the kinds of piece (a schematic, a table, a locking headline). The field, the colours, the faces, the geometry and the components are this motion's own.
